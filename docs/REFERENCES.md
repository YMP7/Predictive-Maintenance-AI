repository in v1.1 because most are publisher-copyrighted and may not be
redistributed. Obtain them through the DOI links or your institution.

**How the DOIs were obtained (unverified):** each DOI was extracted
automatically from the PDF's metadata or from the text of its first two pages
(`pypdf`). A DOI found in the page text can occasionally belong to a cited
paper instead of the paper itself, so verify each link resolves to the listed
title before citing. 23 of 36 PDFs yielded a DOI; the other 13 are marked
and need manual lookup. Numbering follows the original file names and
`docs/LITERATURE_MAPPING.md`.
